# Decisions and open questions

## Topic 12 — exotic propulsion race (6 Oct 2026)

### Positions held
- A US-China propulsion race exists for hypersonics (China ahead) and space nuclear power
  (US ahead on paper schedules). It does not exist in public evidence for exotic physics.
- "More important than AI": rejected. AI is the general enabler; propulsion is one of its
  downstream uses.
- "AI is mainly for propulsion physics": rejected on the public record (neither country's AI
  strategy mentions propulsion). A classified or PLA-doctrine motive was not examined.
- Nuclear-race analogy: fails on immediacy, answerability, size of the jump, and confirmed
  physics.

### Superseded / do-not-quote
- "China tested a Mach 16 detonation engine": it is a design paper. The oblique-detonation
  test was about Mach 9 for about 50 ms in a shock tunnel.
- "China built a 1.5 MW space nuclear reactor": a non-nuclear ground prototype.
- "Declassified files reveal a China-Russia UFO race": a June 2026 NewsNation interview with
  an advocate (Jordan Flowers), not file contents.
- PLA AI-for-UAP story dated "2023" in some write-ups: it is 2021 SCMP reporting, about
  detection and triage.

### Open questions
- Who wrote the tweet?
- FY2027 NDAA conference outcome for the UAP Disclosure Act (House passed the full version
  22 Jul 2026).
- Has HACM flown? What is the total FY2027 hypersonics request?
- Is there a propulsion motive in PLA "intelligentized warfare" writings?
- Has AARO's Historical Record Vol. 2 been published?
- AI for ultra-high-temperature materials and thermal protection: no citable result found.
- What would move tier 3: an independently replicated anomalous-thrust result on a
  high-precision balance, or physical material coming out of the disclosure process.
