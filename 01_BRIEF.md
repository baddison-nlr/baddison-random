## Topic 12 (6 Oct 2026): is exotic propulsion the real US-China race?

**The tweet:** "The most important arms race between China and the US is not AI. AI is
second... The most important race is for advanced/exotic propulsion. The main reason for the
AI race is that it can accelerate the advanced physics required for the new propulsion
systems."

**Verdict: the order is backwards.** "Exotic propulsion" covers three tiers:

1. **Hypersonics and detonation engines: a real race.** China leads in fielding by about five
   years (DF-17, DF-27, YJ-21) and in test tempo. US Dark Eagle got its first battery in late
   2025. Lockheed builds about 6-7 of the 12 missiles needed per year. China's detonation-engine
   headlines are millisecond tunnel runs and design papers. The stakes are theater-level
   (Taiwan, carriers, Guam), not strategic.
2. **Space nuclear power and fusion: a real policy race, but over lunar power, not a drive.**
   The US cancelled DRACO, its nuclear rocket demo, in 2025 over launch costs. That is the
   strongest single fact against the tweet. The US then pivoted to a nuclear-electric Mars
   mission (SR-1 Freedom, Dec 2028) and a lunar reactor by 2030. China and Russia target
   2033-36 for theirs. China outspends the US on public fusion money (about $1.5B vs $0.8B a
   year), but that is a power-plant race.
3. **Genuinely exotic (UAP reverse-engineering, warp, EmDrive): claim/theory.** A 2026
   presidential declassification order, six file releases and lifted NDAs produced only old
   sightings. The WSJ's "Yankee Blue" story documents a decades-long Air Force hazing ritual
   that briefed new officers into a fake alien-tech program. EmDrive, the Mach-effect
   thruster and the Navy's Pais patents all failed precise tests.

**On AI:** AI speeds up the engineering (plasma control, scramjet control, simulation
surrogates, materials screening). Its 2026 physics wins are hard calculations inside known
theory. Neither the US AI Action Plan nor China's AI+ plan mentions propulsion.

**The nuclear analogy fails.** The bomb was immediate, unanswerable, a thousandfold jump, and
built on physics confirmed in 1938. The only tier that would match is the one with no
confirmed physics.

**Who pushes the idea:** the tweet's author was not found. It is closest to Eric Weinstein
(physics stalled and classified, and AIs will read what physicists laughed at), blended with
disclosure advocates' claims that China and Russia are reverse-engineering craft.

**Defensible narrower version:** hypersonics and space nuclear power are underrated next to
the AI hype, and AI will speed up both for whoever has better test infrastructure.
